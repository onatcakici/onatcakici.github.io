Wilco

2026
General purpose helper robot · ARMS Two SO 101 · Ready to build

Wilco is a small general purpose helper robot, designed so that anyone with a 3D printer can build one. An omni wheel base carries a torso with two SO 101 arms, a Raspberry Pi 5 and a head with round screen eyes, a camera, two microphones and a speaker. It runs LeRobot, so it can learn tasks from demonstrations, like tidying a desk or reseating a cable in a server rack.

I designed every part in Fusion for easy manufacture: printed parts plus off the shelf components, with one build guide for the parts, the wiring and the software. Every part, fastener and wire is checked for fit in the model. It weighs about 6.8 kg and stands 0.65 m tall. Wilco has not been built yet; the numbers come from the model.
